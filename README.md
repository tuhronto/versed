# Versed

Synced lyrics in your Discord status. Versed shows the line you're hearing on your profile while you listen in Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL or your browser.

**Download:** [Windows](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/releases/latest/download/Versed-Setup.exe) (10 and 11) · [macOS](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/releases/latest/download/Versed-mac.dmg) (11 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel) · [Linux AppImage](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/releases/latest/download/Versed-linux.AppImage) · [Linux .deb](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/releases/latest/download/Versed-linux.deb) · or see the [website](https://getversed.vercel.app).

![Versed](https://getversed.vercel.app/shots/now.webp)

- Lyrics from LRCLIB and NetEase, paced to Discord's update limit, with song changes showing up right away
- Romanization for Japanese and Korean lyrics, and an all-lowercase option
- Lyrics overlay for games and video, colored by the album art if you like
- Big lyrics: press F to fill the window with just the line being sung
- Pauses on its own during calls, games or hours you choose
- Stats: top songs and artists, listening time, streaks and your most shown lyric line, kept on your PC
- Up to two buttons on your profile, like "Play on Spotify" for the current song
- Lyric cards, custom status layouts, media controls with drag-to-scrub
- A built-in log for bug reports, and it updates itself

**New in 0.4.3:** smooth play and pause, calmer song changes, and a Mac version that looks and behaves like a Mac app. 0.4.2 fixed the slow first-launch permission prompt on macOS, and 0.4.0 brought macOS and Linux versions and lyrics for more songs. See the [release notes](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/releases).

Installed copies update automatically (on Linux, the AppImage does; the .deb is updated by downloading the new one).

The apps aren't code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn: choose More info, then Run anyway. On macOS, open Versed once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. On macOS Versed works with Spotify and Apple Music; on Windows and Linux with any player, browsers included.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/tuhronto/versed/issues).

This repository only hosts releases and the website.
